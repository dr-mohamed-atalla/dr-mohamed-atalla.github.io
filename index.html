About

Ph.D. researcher in Environmental Geomorphology and GIS, with over 16 years of combined academic, research and professional experience. I conduct interdisciplinary research, develop new methods, and combine geospatial technologies, environmental data and computational approaches to address complex scientific and societal challenges.

My expertise spans environmental and physical geography, climate change, hydrology, natural hazards, land-use dynamics and sustainability. I work with GIS, remote sensing, Google Earth Engine, Python, GeoAI, machine learning and spatial modelling, and have a record of peer-reviewed publications, scientific collaborations and university teaching.

I aim to contribute to collaborative research teams and to turn scientific findings into practical solutions for sustainable development and evidence-based decisions.

I am an Esri-certified ArcGIS Enterprise Geodata Management Professional (2025), an associate editor and editorial board member for two journals, and a reviewer for more than 15 international journals.
